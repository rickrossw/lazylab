The Slow Lane

Throughput just halved across 32 GPUs, but nothing failed. Find the straggler.

Briefingclick to collapse

A 7B run is training on 4 nodes (32 H100s), and node-5 is idle as a spare. A few minutes ago tokens/s dropped by ~45%. No errors in the job log. Nothing crashed.

In synchronous data-parallel training, every step waits for the slowest GPU. Find it, take its node out of rotation and get MFU back above 60%.

Tools: the dashboards on the right, nvidia-smi, nvidia-smi -q -d PERFORMANCE, --query-gpu=index,temperature.gpu,clocks.sm, scontrol.
