Two Nodes, One Job

Your first multi-node training job won't even submit. Fix the batch script.

Briefingclick to collapse

A researcher left you train.sh: a 7B training run that should use 2 nodes × 8 H100s. They say "it just errors out." Get it running across both nodes, and make sure the nodes talk over InfiniBand, not the slow management Ethernet.

Useful commands: cat train.sh, nano train.sh, sinfo, sbatch train.sh, squeue, cat slurm-<jobid>.out.
