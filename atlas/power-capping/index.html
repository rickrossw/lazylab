Power capping

Read it in Chapter 5 · checkpoint +50 XP →

Lower a GPU's power limit and give up a little speed for a lot less energy: often 20% less power for under 10% slower.

Near the top of the voltage/frequency curve, power grows much faster than performance: dynamic power ∝ f·V², and voltage must rise with frequency. Capping power therefore costs proportionally less performance than it saves.

In training, only the compute-bound part of each step slows down. Communication, data loading and pipeline bubbles don't care about SM clocks, so the real-world throughput hit is smaller still.

How

sudo nvidia-smi -pl 550            # all GPUs on this node
sudo nvidia-smi -pl 550 -i 3       # just GPU 3

Caps reset on reboot unless managed by your provisioning or by DCGM policies.

Caveats: cap all GPUs in a job. Capping one creates a straggler. And measure tokens per joule, not just tokens per second.
